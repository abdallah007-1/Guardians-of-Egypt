# 🏛️ GUARDIANS OF EGYPT

### Discover. Explore. Preserve.

## About the Project

Guardians of Egypt is a tourism and cultural heritage awareness
campaign developed as part of the DEPI Digital Marketing Specialist
graduation project.

Our mission is to encourage people to discover Egypt's historical
destinations, explore its hidden gems, and understand the importance
of preserving its cultural heritage.

## Our Campaigns

### 1. Egypt Uncovered
Discover lesser-known destinations and hidden gems across Egypt.

### 2. Discover Egypt
Explore verified archaeological discoveries and new tourism
developments.

### 3. Revive Egypt
Raise awareness about archaeological restoration, heritage
preservation, and responsible tourism.
